You find yourself at the Library's centre.  At your feet, innumerable hexagons yawn endlessly into the void.  Above you, no ceiling; only the oppressive hexagonal column, retreating as far as the eye can see.

You tear your eyes from the dizzying sight.  A book on a nearby shelf attracts your attention.  Along its spine, spidery letters trace the words *Axaxaxas Mlö*.  It contains a bewildering array of [pictures][].

Will you leaf through its [pages][]?  Or would you rather peruse [all that the library has to offer][all]?[^broken]

Whispers reach your ears that generations ago, by some blasphemous art, a heretical sect birthed the Indices of [categories][cats] and [tags][] from the Library's incomprehensible catalogue.

Finally, the library's dusty archives can be found [here][archives].[^archive]
