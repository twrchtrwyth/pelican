Baking sourdough bread is remarkably simple: it just takes a bit of planning.  The three key stages are as follows:

1. Prepare the production sourdough
2. Mix the production sourdough with the "soaker" and allow to prove
3. Bake

# Production Sourdough

## Original

| Thing | How Much (g) |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Starter | 75 |
| White flour | 55 |
| Wholemeal flour | 85 |
| Water (35°C) | 85 |


## Alternative

| Thing | How Much (g) |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Starter | 25 |
| White flour | 25 |
| Wholemeal flour | 30 |
| Water (35°C) | 30 |

Leave to ferment for 4--8 hours.


# Soaker

## Original

| Thing | How Much (g) |
| ----- | ------------ |
| White flour | 200 |
| Wholemeal flour | 200 |
| Water (30°C) | 300 |
| Salt | 8 |


## Alternative

| Thing | How Much (g) |
| ----- | ------------ |
| White flour | 250 |
| Wholemeal flour | 250 |
| Water (30°C) | 375 |
| Salt | 8 |

Mix flours and salt and leave for 30 minutes.  Mix in starter then leave to prove for 3---5 hours.


# Bake

| Temperature (°C) | Time (mins) |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| 230 | 10 |
| 210 | 20---25 |

Temperatures are for fan oven.  Could try 240°C then 220°C instead, same times.
