The Fuji X100F is a superb little fixed-lens compact camera, which essentially accompanies me everywhere (particularly since I reverted to using my iPhone SE because I resent that modern phones are the size of chopping boards).  Since camera film became practically unaffordable a few years ago, I have switched almost entirely to digital photography.  I find using any photo manipulation software highly tedious, and so am keen to just get photo processing done in-camera wherever possible.  The X100F offers excellent built-in film simulations of numerous film stocks, but here are a couple of custom simulations that I use routinely.

## Colour

This is the best all-purpose colour simulation I have come across (unfortunately I can't remember where I found it).  It's less intense than Velvia (which it must be said is also excellent), with a lovely film-like quality.

DR 200; WB Auto R+4 B-5; NR -4; Classic Chrome base; H Tone +1; S Tone +1; Color +3; Sharpness 0; Exp comp +1/3--2/3

## Black & White

A great high-contrast black and white look.  For extra oomph, use Acros with the red filter.

DR 200; WB Auto R+4 B-5; NR -3; Acros base; H Tone +4; S Tone +2; Sharpness 0; Exp comp 0
